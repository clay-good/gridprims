## Direction contract

THESIS: Every number arrives the way an equipment rating does: engraved on a plate with its rating basis stamped beside it, permanent and checkable. Refuses the category default of a grey dashboard with one blue accent, and refuses the outgoing highway-sign slabs.

OWN-WORLD: A machined dark shell (ink #17191A, plate #222624) carrying light work surfaces where dense numbers live. One signal orange (#E8491D) owns the measured value, the active section, and nothing else. Archivo, self-hosted, carries display and UI; engraved legends are uppercase at wide tracking in small sizes; all data sits in tabular figures. Depth comes from 1px light-and-dark edge rules only, never bevels, gradients, glass, or glow. Status keeps its own reserved colours (orange screening, amber stale, red refusal, green checked), each with a glyph and a word.

STORY: The visitor sees a measured value at plate scale, reads the basis stamped under it (source, as-of date, validation level), trusts it because the basis travels with the number everywhere it goes, then drops their own file or follows a handoff.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark rail across the top: wordmark, five sections, theme control. The work area opens already working on the sample meter file: peak demand at plate scale on a raised plate, load factor, energy and bill on their own plates beside it, each plate's lower edge a stamped band reading BASIS · source · as-of · validation. A stepped load curve with the peak marked sits under them on a light work surface, and the bill breakdown table below that. The ZIP door and the drop target sit in a right column. Primary action is a filled orange control; everything else is quiet.
