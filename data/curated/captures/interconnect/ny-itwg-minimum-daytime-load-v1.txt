[Title]
CESIR Analysis Evaluation - Minimum Daytime Load Application & Assumptions
[Revision table, Ver 1 row]
Ver 1 | 13 Feb 2024 | Industry | Summary of CESIR Analysis evaluation and Industry Request & questions
[Current Understanding, paragraph 1]
The industry has consistently observed that minimum day time load data is not available for feeders. Based on the frequency of this scenario it is estimated that minimum load data is not available for the majority (70-80%) of the feeders.
[Current Understanding, paragraph 2]
In a scenario where minimum day time load (MDL) data is not available the utilities determine it based on the assumption that minimum day time load is 15%-40% of peak load. The assumption varies across each utility, “Technical Guidance Matrix for Integrating DER - September 2022” documents the different assumptions.
