# data/curated/conformance

Bill test vectors (`vectors/*.json`, one per file, in the format of [docs/formats/bill-test-vector.md](../../../docs/formats/bill-test-vector.md)) and the findings that a vector is wrong (`findings.json`: `{vector, finding, date}`).

The gate in `verify/gates/vectors.mjs` checks every vector, then runs it through the bill engine: a vector the engine misses by more than its tolerance blocks the release until the engine is fixed or a finding shows the vector is wrong. The first release comes from the regulator bill tables: `node verify/tools/ground-truth-vectors.mjs` writes a vector for each schedule whose every itemized charge the engine can read, at 250, 1,000, and 5,000 kWh, and `release.json` with the coverage by charge type (zeros included) and the schedules that could not be written, each with why. Each vector's tariff is validated against the URPX shapes (`verify/reference/urpx/vector-shacl-results.json`), and a test fails if the committed files drift from what the ground truth gives.

Regulator ground truth (`ground-truth/*.json`) holds the typical bills a state commission published, each figure read twice by independent methods, with its page. Which commissions publish one, under what terms, and why each state is used, excluded, not yet extracted, or has no publication is in [`../conformance-publications.mjs`](../conformance-publications.mjs), one record per state and DC. The site publishes both at `/data/ground-truth/`, with the limits of these figures stated before every table and in every download.

Releases (`releases/`) freeze the vectors, the format documentation, the coverage, and the limits in one file per version, `bill-test-vectors-<version>.json`, listed with its SHA-256 in `releases/changelog.json`. Cut one with `node verify/tools/ground-truth-vectors.mjs --cut <year.month.patch> <date> "<what changed>"`; the check fails if a release changes after it is cut or the vectors change without a new one. The site serves each at `/data/ground-truth/vectors/` and the latest as `/data/ground-truth/vectors.json`.

The scorecard compares each regulator-priced schedule with its record in the open tariff database (URDB). `urdb-crosswalk.mjs` says which record each schedule is compared with and why, or why it cannot be. `scorecard-findings.json` holds any maintainer finding from reading the filed tariff, as `{state, utility, schedule, verdict, reader, date, finding, reported?: {date, reference}}`. Until there is one, a disagreement is worded as a disagreement between the two sources. The site publishes the comparison at `/data/ground-truth/scorecard/`, with counts and distributions beside our own engine's, and no composite score.

The scorecard is reproducible: `/data/ground-truth/scorecard/scorecard.json` states its method and the SHA-256 of every input, and `rates.csv` and `results.csv` list the rates and every compared bill. To rerun it independently, run `node verify/tools/scorecard-rates.mjs > /tmp/rates.json`, then `python3 -I verify/tools/scorecard-rerun.py data/urdb/2026-10/rates.csv.gz /tmp/rates.json`, which prices each record from the raw URDB rows with no gridprims code. Its stored output is `verify/reference/scorecard/urdb-rerun.json`.
